Why this isn't a rule yet

  • The funnel numbers are per stock, and WYNN and CCL each appear twice, so the 7 trades are really 5 stocks and the 16 real trades are about 8. With about 70 measures tested, several will line up by luck.
  • The before-entry measures that separate the groups are mostly volatility measured several ways: the LSTM error grows with volatility, landing within ±2% is easier for a calm stock, and volatile stocks produce bigger backtest and sim dollar numbers.
  • DDOG, the most volatile name, went the other way in other books: its copy-mode puts peaked +325% and +604%. With those included the MAE link drops to -0.36.
  • A larger LSTM error did go with more volatile stocks, but that did not give bigger option peaks here, because options on those stocks were already priced for it (implied vol 41 to 77% against 18 to 34%).

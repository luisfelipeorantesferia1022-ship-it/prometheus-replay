The sample

What it means

  • Calmer, lower-error stocks did not give bigger option peaks. Across all options the higher-error fifth peaked slightly higher, not lower; holding to the Thursday exit showed no difference at all.
  • The reason: calm stocks have cheaper options with more leverage, but they also move less, and the two cancel out. Sorted by implied vol, the median peak is flat.
  • Once implied vol is known, LSTM error adds nothing (partial correlation +0.04).
  • Among the stage-1 candidates alone, the names the funnel actually trades, there is no relationship either way.
  • Calls on more volatile stocks did better in 2025, a rising year; puts showed no such pattern. That is the market's direction, not calmness.
  • The earlier pattern (calmer names gave the big peaks) came from 7 trades on 5 stocks and does not hold up.
